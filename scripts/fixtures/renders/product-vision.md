# Product Vision: Ledger

## Vision

Ledger is the place a small finance team trusts to say what happened to a payment, without asking an engineer.

## Problem Statement

Reconciliation lives in spreadsheets rebuilt every month, so the answer to "why is this invoice open" depends on who is asked.

## Target Users & Personas

- **Controller** – owns the month-end close; needs a defensible trail more than a dashboard.
- **Support lead** – answers customer payment questions under a response-time promise.

## Value Propositions

- One queryable history per payment, from authorization to settlement.
- Month-end close that does not require a data export.

## Anti-Goals

- Not a general BI tool; no arbitrary chart builder.
- Not a payment processor; Ledger never moves money.

## Roadmap Themes

- Reconciliation first, reporting second.
- Read paths before write paths.

## Open Questions

- Which ledger of record wins when the processor and the bank disagree?
